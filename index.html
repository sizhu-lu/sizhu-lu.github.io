Welcome to my homepage!

I am postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University, where I am very fortunate to work with Professors Lihua Lei, Guido Imbens, and Kevin Chen.

Before joining Stanford, I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Department of Statistics at UC Berkeley, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Professor Peng Ding. I was also very fortunate to be advised by Professor Ting Ye on some projects. Previously, I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics and B.A. in Finance from Peking University. I also spent time working in the world of finance and real estate after college and received my Ph.D. in Business Administration from Haas School of Business, UC Berkeley, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Professor Amir Kermani. Here are links to my Google Scholar profile and GitHub.

I will join the Department of Statistics & Data Science at Carnegie Mellon University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Fall 2027.
